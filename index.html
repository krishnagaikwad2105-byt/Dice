चंपाळ पाणी (Champal Pani) — Guide & Rules

The Ancestral Indian Strategy Game of Maharashtra

🎯 Objective

Be the first player to guide all 4 pawns (सोंगट्या) around the board, through the inner water stream ("पाणी"), and into the central winning square (स्वर्ग / Swarga).

🐚 The 4 Cowrie Shells (कवड्या)

Players throw 4 cowrie shells on each turn. The count of shells landing face-up (mouth showing) determines the move:

  • 1 Open: Move 1 space.
  • 2 Open: Move 2 spaces.
  • 3 Open: Move 3 spaces.
  • 4 Open: Move 4 spaces + Earn an Extra Roll!
  • 0 Open (All 4 Mouths Down): Move 8 spaces (Champool / चंपाळ!) + Earn an Extra Roll!

🌊 The Core "Pani" (पाण्यात जाणे) Rule

This rule defines the Maharashtrian identity of the game!

  • Pawns begin at their home sanctuary and travel counter-clockwise around the 16 outer perimeter squares.
  • A player CANNOT enter the inner track ("Pani") until that player has captured ("killed") at least one opponent pawn!
  • If you haven't killed an enemy yet, your pawn will loop around the outer ring again, continuing the hunt.
  • Once you capture an opponent pawn, the "PANI" stream is unlocked for all your pawns! From step 15, they turn inward into the clockwise inner ring toward Swarga.

⚔️ Capturing & Safe Sanctuaries

  • Safe Squares (घरे): The 4 starting outer middle squares and the center square are marked with an ✕. Pawns on safe squares can never be captured and can coexist peacefully.
  • Capturing: Landing on an opponent's single pawn on any unmarked square captures ("kills") it! The captured pawn is sent back to its starting base, and the attacker earns a Bonus Turn!

🏆 Entering Swarga (Center Home)

To enter the central Swarga square, an exact roll is required. A pawn that reaches Swarga has completed its sacred journey!
